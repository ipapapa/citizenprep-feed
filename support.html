Frequently asked

  • Which test should I study? Applicants who filed Form N-400 on or after Oct 20, 2025 take the 2025 test (128 questions); before that, the 2008 test (100 questions). Pick yours in Settings.
  • Why do some answers say "Live"? Answers that change with elections (President, your senators, governor, and more) update automatically so you never study outdated officials.
  • How do I see my representative? Enter your ZIP code in Settings. It's used only to look up your U.S. Representative and never leaves your device.
  • What does CitizenPrep Pro do? Everything you need to pass is free. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads.
  • Restoring a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchases.
